# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential freelance clients — product owners, startup founders, or small-business leads — who are evaluating whether Nélio Dias is the right developer to build their web project. They arrive with intent: they're not browsing, they're vetting. They want to see range, craft, and reliability. Secondary: technical peers or recruiters who find the portfolio through a link or search.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Nélio Dias, a full-stack developer based in Brazil. It exists to generate freelance leads by converting a first-time visitor into a confident contact. Success looks like: the visitor leaves with a clear understanding of Nélio's technical scope, a sense of trust in his reliability, and a low-friction path to reaching out.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, reliable, modern. Calm confidence — not quiet by default, but quiet by choice. The restraint is deliberate; every element earns its place. The portfolio itself demonstrates the developer's standard.

## Anti-references

- Generic shadcn/ui or SaaS-kit defaults (the portfolio should not look like every other Next.js dev's output)
- Cheerful startup-energy portfolios (loud gradients, bouncy animations, "Let's build something amazing!" copy)
- Identical card grids used as a crutch (three cards in a row is not a Services section)
- Heavy decoration as a substitute for clear communication

## Design Principles

1. **Let the work speak** — projects are the primary content; the interface frames them, never competes.
2. **Precision over decoration** — every spacing choice, type size, and color serves a purpose; none are there for aesthetics alone.
3. **Trust through clarity** — freelance clients need to believe you communicate well; the interface should model that.
4. **Show, don't describe** — the design quality of the portfolio is itself a portfolio piece.
5. **Quiet authority** — restraint signals confidence; noise signals insecurity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. Reduced-motion support already in place (respect `prefers-reduced-motion`). Dark/light mode supported. Content in pt-BR.
